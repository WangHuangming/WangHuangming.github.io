MULTI-AGENT SYSTEMS01

MATS Harness

Let agents reason. Keep execution in check.

A crypto trading research harness with team-based ReAct loops, reusable strategy skills, and a trace of every tool call. Backtesting, paper trading, and controlled live execution each have their own boundaries.

PythonReAct / LangGraphMongoDBReact
Under the hood+

I refactored a fixed multi-agent workflow into per-team tool loops with a tool registry and modular skill loading. The CLI and dashboard expose traces, tool replay, portfolio state, and order records.

The engineering focus: explicit live confirmation, halt controls, risk checks, and persistent order ledgers.

RAG · FULL-STACK APPLICATION02

LawGPT

Legal documents in. Context-aware answers out.

A legal AI assistant that brings uploaded documents into the conversation. I built the path from PDF ingestion and vector retrieval to persistent chats and a responsive React interface.

React / ReduxExpress.jsQdrantLangChain
Under the hood+

PDFs are parsed, chunked, embedded, and stored in Qdrant. Relevant passages are retrieved into model context, with routing across OpenAI, Gemini, Groq, and local Ollama models.

Beyond the chat box: authentication, email verification, password reset, protected routes, and conversation history.

voice → context → structure
LOCAL AI · SPEECH PIPELINE03

GridScribe

A better listener for the power grid.

A privacy-conscious API that turns inspection audio into validated, structured records. Local Whisper transcription meets domain-specific RAG and Ollama correction.

FastAPIWhisperOllamaPydantic
Under the hood+

Audio validation and ffmpeg preprocessing feed a local transcription, retrieval, correction, and JSON validation pipeline. Human-reviewed examples are prepared for future SFT/QLoRA; unreviewed model outputs are not treated as training labels.

KNOWLEDGE SYSTEMS · RAG04

RAG Wiki

Knowledge you can read, edit, and trace.

A local, Markdown-first knowledge system that turns source documents into maintainable, cited pages. Every proposed change goes through Plan, Preview, and Apply.

MarkdownSource provenanceNeo4j
Under the hood+

Staged extraction, page planning, and drafting keep the workflow understandable. Source hashing, citation validation, stale-plan detection, transactional writes, and rollback make changes inspectable and recoverable. An optional Neo4j projection adds a graph view.
